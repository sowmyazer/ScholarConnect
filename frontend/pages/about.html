About ScholarConnect

An AI-powered scholarship portal built to ensure no deserving student misses a scholarship opportunity due to lack of awareness.

❗ The Problem

Millions of Students Miss Scholarships Every Year

In India, thousands of government scholarships go unclaimed every year — not because students are ineligible, but because they simply don't know about them. Government school students from rural and semi-urban areas face the biggest gap in scholarship awareness.

The application process involves multiple documents, state portals, deadlines scattered across months, and complex eligibility criteria that are hard to navigate without guidance.

Our Objectives

ScholarConnect is built with a clear mission — to bridge the gap between scholarships and students

🔍

Centralize Discovery

Aggregate all central and state government scholarships in one searchable platform so students don't have to visit multiple websites.

🤖

AI-Powered Matching

Use intelligent algorithms to instantly match students to scholarships based on their category, income, class, and academic performance.

⏰

Deadline Awareness

Send real-time notifications and countdown timers so students never miss an application deadline again.

📋

Document Guidance

Provide clear step-by-step guides for each document required, including where to get them and how to use them.

🌐

Multilingual Support

Support Telugu and English to ensure the platform is accessible to students in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

📱

Mobile-First Design

Designed to work perfectly on low-end Android phones and 2G connections used by most government school students.
