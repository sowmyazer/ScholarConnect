📋 Documents Guide

Complete guide to all documents required for scholarship applications. Know what to collect, where to get them, and how to use them.

⚠️
Before You Apply

Always keep 2–3 photocopies of each document. Ensure all documents are attested by a Gazetted Officer or School Principal where required. Check validity dates on income certificates (usually valid for 1 year).

📝 Complete Documents Checklist

Keep all these documents ready before applying to any scholarship

☑️ Income Certificate

🪪

Aadhaar Card

Required for ALL scholarships
📌 Purpose

Identity proof and biometric verification. Used to link bank account for direct benefit transfer (DBT).

📋 Required Information

12-digit Aadhaar number, registered mobile number linked to Aadhaar, bank account must be seeded with Aadhaar.

🏢 How to Obtain
  1. Visit nearest Aadhaar Seva Kendra or Post Office
  2. Carry original address and identity proof
  3. Biometric enrollment takes 10 minutes
  4. Card arrives by post in 90 days. Download at uidai.gov.in
💰

Income Certificate

Valid for 1 year
📌 Purpose

Proves family's annual income is within the scholarship's income limit. Most critical document for need-based scholarships.

📋 Required Information

Annual family income from all sources, names of earning members, certificate issued by Tahsildar / MRO.

🏢 How to Obtain
  1. Visit Tahsildar / MRO office or Mee-Seva / Jan Seva Kendra
  2. Fill application form with family income details
  3. Attach salary slips / agriculture details
  4. Collect certificate within 7–15 working days
📜

Caste Certificate

SC / ST / BC / OBC
📌 Purpose

Proves belonging to Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe, or Backward Class community for category-specific scholarships.

📋 Required Information

Caste name, community certificate number, date of issue. Issued by competent authority (Tahsildar).

🏢 How to Obtain
  1. Apply at Tahsildar's office with ration card and school records
  2. Or apply online at state MeeSeva / eSeva portal
  3. Submit caste declaration form
  4. Certificate issued within 15 days
🏫

Study Certificate

From School Principal
📌 Purpose

Confirms the student is currently enrolled in school. Most scholarships require this to be issued for the current academic year.

📋 Required Information

Student name, class, roll number, academic year, school name and DISE code, principal's signature & seal.

🏢 How to Obtain
  1. Request from your School Principal or Headmaster
  2. Usually provided free of cost within 1–2 days
  3. Must have current academic year mentioned
  4. Should have school seal and principal signature
🏅

Bonafide Certificate

For Higher Education
📌 Purpose

Official proof from institution confirming student's bona fide enrollment. Often needed for higher education and college scholarships.

📋 Required Information

Student name, course, enrollment number, academic year, institution letterhead with authorized signature.

🏢 How to Obtain
  1. Apply in writing to school/college administration
  2. May require nominal fee at some institutions
  3. Issued within 3–5 working days
  4. Valid for 6 months from date of issue
🏦

Bank Passbook

For DBT Transfer
📌 Purpose

Bank account details for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of scholarship amount. Account must be in student's own name.

📋 Required Information

Account holder name, account number, IFSC code, bank name and branch, Aadhaar-linked status (mandatory).

🏢 How to Obtain
  1. Open account at any nationalized bank (SBI, PNB, etc.) or Post Office
  2. Zero-balance accounts available for students under PMJDY
  3. Link Aadhaar to bank account (mandatory for DBT)
  4. Get front page photocopy showing IFSC and account number
📷

Passport Size Photo

Recent (3 months)
📌 Purpose

Identity verification on application form. Recent photos (within 3 months) must be used.

📋 Required Information

White background, clear face photo, size 3.5cm × 4.5cm, minimum 6 copies recommended. School uniform preferred.

🏢 How to Obtain
  1. Visit local photo studio
  2. Costs approximately ₹30–₹60 for 12 copies
  • Wear school uniform for official applications
  • Ensure white or light background as specified

    Previous Year Marks Card

    For Merit Scholarships
    📌 Purpose

    Shows academic performance for merit-based and renewal of scholarships. Required for NMMS and INSPIRE scholarships.

    📋 Required Information

    Student name, marks in all subjects, total marks and percentage, class and school details, board/examination details.

    🏢 How to Obtain
    1. Available from school/board after results declared
    2. For State Board: collect from school or download online
    3. Get attested photocopy for applications
    4. Keep original safe, use photocopies for submissions
    📃

    EWS Certificate

    Economically Weaker Section
    📌 Purpose

    Certifies that a General category student belongs to the Economically Weaker Section (income below ₹8 lakh per year).

    📋 Required Information

    Family's annual income (all sources), family assets declaration, no property above specified limits, General category affirmation.

    🏢 How to Obtain
    1. Apply at Tahsildar or Sub-Divisional Magistrate's office
    2. Submit Form I with supporting documents
    3. Income proof, property documents required
    4. Valid for 1 year from issue date

    💡 Pro Tips for Document Preparation

    📁

    Create a Document Folder

    Keep a physical folder with 3 copies of each document. Label each clearly. This saves time when applying to multiple scholarships.

    📱

    Digitize Your Documents

    Scan or photograph all documents at 300 DPI. Save as PDF. Keep a Google Drive/DigiLocker backup for online applications.

    ⏰

    Check Validity Dates

    Income certificate valid 1 year. EWS certificate 1 year. Caste certificate permanent. Check before each application season.
