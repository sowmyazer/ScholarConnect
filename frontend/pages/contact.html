❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a scholarship? ▼
Use our Eligibility Checker to find matching scholarships, then click "Apply Now" to go to the official portal. Keep your documents ready before applying.
Is ScholarConnect free to use? ▼
Yes, ScholarConnect is completely free for all students. We do not charge any fees for finding scholarships, checking eligibility, or using any feature on this platform.
What documents do I need? ▼
Most scholarships require: Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate, Caste Certificate, Study Certificate, Bank Passbook, and Passport Photo. Visit our Documents Guide for full details.
Can I apply for multiple scholarships? ▼
Yes, you can apply to multiple scholarships as long as you meet the eligibility criteria for each. However, some scholarships may have restrictions on holding multiple scholarships simultaneously.
My income certificate expired. What to do? ▼
Income certificates are valid for 1 year. Visit your Tahsildar's office or MeeSeva/Jan Seva Kendra to get a fresh one. It usually takes 7–15 working days.
